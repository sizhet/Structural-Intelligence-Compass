# [0.1.0] — 2026-08-19

## Initial Public Research Release

Initial release of the **Structural Intelligence Compass**, a compact and open research framework for navigating major questions, structures, engineering directions, and future frontiers across the Structural Intelligence research landscape.

The release establishes the **12-Point Compass**, a candidate **Minimal Structural Basis for Broad Intelligence**, and a methodology for challenging that basis through **Basis Stress Testing** and **Residual Intelligence**.

The repository is intentionally presented as:

> **A compass, not a closed map.**

## Added — Residual-Based Coverage Confidence

Introduced **Residual-Based Coverage Confidence** as a conservative way to discuss the apparent breadth of the current Structural Intelligence basis.

The principle is:

> **Confidence in structural coverage increases when increasingly diverse stress tests fail to expose stable unexplained residuals.**

This does not imply completeness.

In particular:

> **No Residual Found ≠ No Residual Exists**

Coverage confidence therefore remains conditional on:

- tested domains;
- available perspectives;
- current representations;
- current operators;
- current evidence.

This connects the Minimal Structural Basis directly back to the Compass principle of open epistemic boundaries.

# Research Position at v0.1.0

This release does **not** claim that:

- the 12-Point Compass is exhaustive;
- the current Structural Intelligence basis is complete;
- exactly three major compression structures are fundamental;
- no additional first-class primitive exists;
- Structural Intelligence replaces LLMs, World Models, or other AI paradigms;
- Hybrid Intelligence eliminates epistemic boundaries;
- AGI implies access to absolute truth.

Instead, v0.1.0 establishes a candidate structural landscape and makes it available for independent examination.

The repository explicitly invites:

- applications;
- counterexamples;
- alternative decompositions;
- cross-domain stress tests;
- residual discovery;
- structural revision.

# Collective Learning

The initial release follows a **Collective Learning** principle.

The objective is not only to publish conclusions.

It is to expose:

- the structural vocabulary;
- the research path;
- the algorithms and mechanisms;
- the candidate basis;
- the known boundaries;
- the method for challenging the basis;
- the path toward deeper source research.

A useful external contribution may therefore be either:

**successful application of the current basis**

or:

**a stable residual showing where the current basis fails**.

Both advance the research.

# Closing Statement

The Structural Intelligence Compass begins by asking what intelligence can legitimately claim.

It then examines the structures through which intelligence may organize, preserve, compress, call, trigger, execute, and validate computation.

Finally, it turns the same structural discipline back onto itself.

The resulting research loop is:

**Observe**

→ **Organize**

→ **Construct**

→ **Stress-Test**

→ **Search for Residuals**

→ **Revise**

→ **Continue**

The Compass therefore remains intentionally open.

> **The territory has authority over the Compass.**

> **A compass, not a closed map.**

> **Small basis. Large composition space. Open boundary.**
